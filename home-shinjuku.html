🔑 How to Get the Key
1

Complete the Check-in Form

Our property uses a self check-in system. To ensure a smooth arrival, please complete the online check-in form in advance.

  • Click the check-in link sent through your booking platform.
    If you booked through another channel, the link can also be found in your reservation email.
  • Enter your self check-in code manually (copied text may not be recognized properly).
  • All guests staying at the property must complete their information.
  • On the day of check-in, click "Check-in Instructions" to view the entry guide.
⚠️ If information for all guests has not been submitted, you will not be able to access the "Check-in Instructions" page.
2

Enter the Entrance Password

After successfully submitting the check-in form, you will receive the entrance door password.

Enter the password on the keypad and press the confirmation button to access the property.

📋 House Rules

Please provide the correct number of guests staying at the property.

  • If the actual number of guests exceeds the reservation details, additional charges will apply.
  • An extra fee of ¥5,000 / additional guest will be charged.
  • Smoking is strictly prohibited in all areas, including balconies. This includes electronic cigarettes and vaping devices.
  • If any evidence of smoking is found, an additional cleaning fee of ¥10,000 will be charged.
  • Pets are not allowed.

We provide towels according to the number of guests and nights reserved. For guests staying 3 nights or longer, room cleaning is available during your stay.

  • Please inform us at least one day in advance to arrange cleaning.
  • Cleaning staff will clean rooms between 10:00 AM – 12:00 PM.
  • Before leaving your room, place the "Please Clean the Room" card on the door.

Please do not dispose of garbage outside the property. Separate garbage into two categories:

  • Food waste
  • Other waste (bottles, cans, paper, etc.)
  • Batteries and lighters must not be thrown into trash bins — leave them on the table and we will handle them.
  • We are unable to dispose of oversized garbage larger than 30 cm. Additional disposal fees may apply if left in the room.
  • Coffee, tea, and bottled water in the room are complimentary.
  • Please turn off lights and electrical appliances when leaving the room.
  • Please handle furniture and appliances with care. Guests may be charged for damages beyond normal wear and tear.
  • Please avoid staining bedding or furniture. Failure to clean may result in an additional cleaning fee of ¥10,000.
  • Illegal activities and use of illegal substances are strictly prohibited. We reserve the right to pursue legal action.
  • According to Japanese law, guests under 18 years old are not permitted to stay alone without a parent or guardian.
